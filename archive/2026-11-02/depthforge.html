Select the correct package for your operating system and Blender version. High-resolution video depth estimation can require approximately 4–5 GB of available memory. macOS 14+ is required for direct video decoding on Apple Silicon; image sequences can be used on older versions.

GETTING STARTED

Install. Import. Create.

01. Download the package matching your OS and Blender version.
02. Open Blender → Preferences → Get Extensions → Install from Disk.
03. Open the 3D Viewport sidebar (N) → DepthForge.
04. Select an image or video and generate your relief.
05. Customize the result using live Geometry Nodes controls.

Important notes

AI depth estimation produces relative depth rather than metric-accurate geometry. Results vary depending on image content, motion, lighting, and depth ambiguity. The video model prioritizes temporal consistency but cannot guarantee flicker-free output for every scene. Processing performance depends on your CPU, available memory, input resolution, and clip duration.
