02 — WORKS ON ANY MESH
One preset, many results

Surface Forge is designed to work on virtually any mesh or object in Blender. Apply the presets to hard-surface models, props, abstract objects, sci-fi assets, product renders, motion graphics elements, characters, primitives and custom geometry.

The same preset can create completely different results depending on the shape of the mesh, giving you a flexible procedural workflow for many different projects.

03 — CUSTOMIZABLE CONTROLS
Switch presets, tweak the look

Each preset includes easy-to-use parameters directly in the modifier panel, so you can adjust the look quickly without editing the node tree.

04 — ASSET BROWSER READY
Browse, apply, refine

All presets are prepared for Blender's Asset Browser with custom thumbnails. Browse the library, select a preset and apply it to your objects in a few clicks.

Surface Forge works with Blender's material system. Apply your own materials directly to the object and create completely different looks with metal, ceramic, glass, plastic, emissive, procedural or custom PBR materials.

The workflow stays procedural and editable. Change presets, adjust parameters, modify materials and keep refining the result at any time.
