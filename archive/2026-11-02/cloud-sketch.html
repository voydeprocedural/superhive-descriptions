BLENDER ADD-ON · V0.3.0

Cloud Sketch

Draw a shape in the 3D viewport and it becomes a volumetric cloud. Turn any 3D model into a cloud, pick from 68 library clouds, or fill the whole sky in one click.

Blender 5.0+ Cycles · EEVEE No baking, no VDBs Everything keyframable

DRAW CLOUDS

If you can draw it, you can render it

Most cloud tools hand you someone else's shapes. Cloud Sketch lets you draw the silhouette you actually want: a heart for a wedding shot, a rabbit for a children's film, a towering cumulus to frame a mountain.

01 · Draw. Click Draw Clouds and sketch a closed shape over your scene. Orbit or zoom between strokes and keep drawing.

02 · Release. The outline is inflated into 3D. Wide areas get deep and round, thin parts stay slender, the way real clouds read.

03 · Tweak. Puffiness, detail, wisps, softness and density update live in the viewport, and every one can be keyframed.

Puffiness, Detail, Softness and Grow, changed live in the Rendered viewport.

MODEL TO CLOUD · NEW IN 0.3

Turn any 3D model into a cloud

Select meshes, text objects or curves and click Model to Cloud. Each model is hidden and a cloud of its shape takes its place.

  • Stays linked. The cloud follows the model when you edit, pose or animate it.
  • Swell keeps thin parts from breaking up; Resolution keeps small parts from going missing.
  • Works for logos and lettering, characters, creatures and props.

SKY BUILDER · NEW IN 0.3

Fill the whole sky in one click

Pick a weather, set Coverage and click Build Sky. Clouds, sun, atmosphere and haze are set up around your active camera.

ANY SHAPE

Your silhouette, kept

Concave, spiky or round, the cloud keeps the outline you drew. Shapes are inflated with a Poisson solve instead of a flat extrusion, so a circle becomes a sphere and a long limb becomes a tube.

LIVE CONTROLS

Art-direct every cloud

The shader reads each value straight away, so nothing needs rebaking.

  • Shape: Puffiness, Detail, Wisps, Billow Size, Streak, Height Shaping
  • Look: Density, Softness, Seed
  • Motion: Grow and Evolve, keyframable like everything else

ANIMATION

Clouds that form, boil and drift

  • Animate Formation keyframes selected clouds growing out of thin air, with an optional stagger.
  • Evolve makes the billows slowly boil.
  • Drift slides the high cloud layers with the wind.
  • Animate Day moves the sun from sunrise to sunset over the frame range.

SUN & ATMOSPHERE

Light the sky live

  • Time of Day moves the sun along its daily path. Or click Point Sun and the sun follows your mouse.
  • Atmosphere presets: Clear, Deep Blue, Hazy, Fiery and Pastel, with Air, Dust, Ozone and colour tint controls.
  • High Clouds: fibrous cirrus and small cloudlets high above the rest.
  • Haze fades distant clouds into the horizon, with an automatic colour.

Everything updates live in the Rendered viewport. Your own HDRI and lights work too.

BUILT FOR PRODUCTION

Light on your scene

  • Fast to render. Each cloud sits in a tight bounding mesh, so Cycles only marches where the cloud can be.
  • Nothing to cache. No VDB files or bakes to keep track of. The shader calculates everything at render time.
  • Any scale. Draw a 5 m cloud for a tabletop shot or a 2 km one for a landscape. Density adapts to the size.
  • Readable in Solid mode. Each cloud shows as a soft white pillow, so you can lay out a sky without rendering.
  • Cycles and EEVEE. Cycles gives the softest light; EEVEE works for previews and stylised shots.
  • One-click setup. Set Up Sky adds a physical sky and sun, and sets volume bounces and EEVEE volume quality for you.

What's included

  • cloud_sketch-0.3.0.zip: the extension, with the cloud library. Install it from Blender's preferences without unzipping it.
  • Cloud_Sketch_0.3.0_Demo_and_Docs.zip: the scene from the featured image (packed and ready to render), a quick-start guide and the license.

Requirements

  • Blender 5.0 or newer (tested on 5.0, 5.1 and 5.2 LTS)
  • Cycles is recommended for final renders. A GPU (OptiX, CUDA, HIP or Metal) makes volumes much faster.

Install

Edit › Preferences › Get Extensions › Install from Disk, then press N in the viewport and open the Cloud Sketch tab.
