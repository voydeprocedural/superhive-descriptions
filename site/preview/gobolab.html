01 — Patterned light
Light that adds atmosphere

Cast sharp window frames across an interior, soften a backdrop with leaf shadows, or add flowing caustic patterns to a stylized composition.

The projected patterns follow the surfaces in your scene, extending naturally across walls, floors, furniture and characters. Explore lighting ideas for architectural visualization, product renders, environment art and cinematic shots.

04 — Alignment
Built to stay aligned
Move the light, keep the pattern
The gobo plane is parented to its Spot Light, so moving or rotating the light keeps the projection setup together.
Change the angle without rebuilding
The gobo plane resizes automatically when you change the spot angle, keeping the light cone covered.
Control the softness
Use a smaller light radius for crisp shadows or a larger radius for softer transitions.
Keep the projector out of the shot
The gobo plane is hidden from the camera and reflections, while remaining part of the lighting setup.
05 — Workflow
From the GoboLab panel
1Open the 3D Viewport sidebar with N and locate GoboLab.
2Click Load Gobo Light and choose a preset using the visual preview.
3Click Import Gobo Light to add the selected setup.
4Aim the light and adjust its settings to suit your scene.
06 — Workflow
From the Asset Browser
1Add the supplied library folder under Preferences > File Paths > Asset Libraries.
2Browse the rendered previews and drag a gobo into your scene.
3To edit the individual objects, disable Instance in the import panel that appears in the bottom-left corner.
4Position the light and adjust Power, Angle and Radius.
07 — Lighting ideas
A few starting points
Sharp window shadows
Start with a radius of around 0.01-0.02 m, then adjust for your scene scale.
Sunlit look
Try a warm light around 4500 K at a low angle.
Layered compositions
Combine different gobo lights to build a more layered composition.
08 — Details
Requirements
Blender
Blender 5.0 or newer
Tested in Blender 5.2
Cycles & EEVEE
Black-and-white gobos project light patterns in both Cycles and EEVEE
Full-color stained-glass projections require Cycles. In EEVEE, these presets project their pattern in grayscale
Soft gradients, including clouds and caustics, may look grainy in EEVEE at low sample counts. Try 128-256 Render Samples to improve the result
