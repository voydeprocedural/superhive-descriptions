Smart Scatter Kit

Smart Environment Scattering

Build Detailed Environments Faster

Smart Scatter Kit is a Geometry Nodes-based scattering system designed for fast, flexible, and controlled environment creation.

Distribute complete asset collections across any mesh using surface-based controls or concentrate assets within specific areas using a proximity controller.

From natural forest floors to abandoned industrial areas and urban waste scenes, the kit provides both the scattering system and ready-to-use environment assets.

Two Flexible Distribution Modes

Surface Normal Distribution

Control where assets appear according to the direction and angle of the target surface. Scatter assets across upward-facing, vertical, or angled areas with greater precision.

Proximity Falloff

Use an Empty object as a controller and position it anywhere in the scene. Assets are distributed around the selected area, allowing you to art-direct localized clusters without editing the target mesh.

Included Asset Collections

Forest Floor Clutter

Plants, flowers, branches, organic debris, rocks, weeds, and natural ground details for forests and overgrown environments.

Industrial Construction Debris

Brick ruins, construction materials, pipes, valves, pallets, containers, tools, and industrial site clutter.

Urban Trash & Litter

Garbage, discarded packaging, street waste, damaged objects, and urban debris for abandoned or polluted scenes.

Simple Workflow

  1. Add the Smart Scatter Kit modifier to your target mesh.
  2. Select one of the included asset collections or use your own collection.
  3. Choose the preferred distribution mode.
  4. Adjust the random seed and scale variation.
  5. For proximity scattering, assign an Empty and move it to the desired area.

Use Your Own Assets

The system is not limited to the included collections. Assign your own Blender collection and use Smart Scatter Kit as a reusable scattering tool for rocks, plants, debris, props, foliage, or other environment assets.
