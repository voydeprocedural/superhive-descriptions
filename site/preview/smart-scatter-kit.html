Smart Scatter Kit
Smart Environment Scattering

Build detailed environments faster.

Smart Scatter Kit is a Geometry Nodes scattering system for fast, flexible and controlled environment creation.

Distribute complete asset collections across any mesh with surface-based controls, or concentrate assets in specific areas with a proximity controller.

From natural forest floors to abandoned industrial areas and urban waste scenes, the kit provides both the scattering system and ready-to-use environment assets.

02 — HOW IT WORKS
Two flexible distribution modes

Surface Normal Distribution controls where assets appear according to the direction and angle of the target surface, so you can scatter across upward-facing, vertical or angled areas with greater precision.

Proximity Falloff uses an Empty object as a controller. Position it anywhere in the scene and assets are distributed around that area, so you can art-direct localized clusters without editing the target mesh.

03 — INCLUDED ASSETS
