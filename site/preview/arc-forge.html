Arc Forge
Procedural Electric Arc Generator

Generate animated electric arcs around any mesh object in seconds.

Arc Forge is a procedural Geometry Nodes generator for Blender that creates dynamic electric arc effects directly around your objects.

It is designed for sci-fi renders, energy shields, magical effects, product visuals, motion graphics and VFX scenes. The system adapts to mesh surfaces automatically and gives you simple controls for arc size, thickness, flicker speed, movement range, material and random seed variation.
