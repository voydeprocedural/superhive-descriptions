02 — HOW IT WORKS
From preset to render-ready
1Pick a terrain from the built-in preset library.
2Hit Import - displacement and subdivision are set automatically.
3The shader applies itself - no node setup, no manual linking.
4Tweak or override - adjust the sliders or swap in your own textures at any time.
