Freezing Effect
Procedural Ice & Frost Modifier

Transform any mesh into a frozen, ice-covered surface in seconds.

Freezing Effect is a procedural Geometry Nodes modifier for Blender that generates ice buildup, frost, surface crystals and hanging icicles directly on your mesh.

The effect is driven by a separate controller object, so you control where the freezing starts, how far it spreads and how the frozen area evolves over time.

02 — HOW IT WORKS
Freeze outward from a controller

Freezing Effect uses a controller object to calculate the freezing area across the mesh surface. Areas closer to the controller freeze first, while areas farther away stay less affected or unfrozen, depending on your settings.

Move or animate the controller to create spreading freeze effects, magical ice transformations, frozen props, winter environments and cinematic object reveals. No baking, no sculpting and no manual texture painting.
