Voyde Nature Starter Pack is a lightweight and easy-to-use collection of stylized natural assets designed for fast scene building.

This pack includes a variety of trees, bushes, grass, flowers and rocks that work perfectly for stylized environments, game scenes, and background setups.

All assets are organized into a simple and clean panel for quick access inside Blender.

Included in the pack:

• 9 stylized trees

• 6 bush variations

• 4 grass assets

• 3 flower models

• 7 mossy rock variations


Features:

• Lightweight geometry

• Clean topology

• Organized asset panel

• Ready-to-use materials

• Optimized for real-time and game scenes


Software:

Blender 4.0 and above


Render Engines:

Cycles and Eevee


Ideal for:

• Stylized scenes

• Background set dressing

• Quick prototyping
