Arc Forge — Procedural Electric Arc Generator

Generate animated electric arcs around any mesh object in seconds.

Arc Forge is a procedural Geometry Nodes generator for Blender that creates dynamic electric arc effects directly around your objects.

It is designed for sci-fi renders, energy shields, magical effects, product visuals, motion graphics, and VFX scenes. The system automatically adapts to mesh surfaces and gives you simple controls for arc size, thickness, flicker speed, movement range, material, and random seed variation.

Key Features

  • Fully procedural Geometry Nodes setup
  • Animated electric arc generation
  • Works with any mesh object
  • Adjustable arc thickness and size
  • Custom flicker speed control
  • Arc speed range controls
  • Material slot support
  • Seed-based random variations
  • Non-destructive workflow
  • Asset Browser ready


Included Controls

  • Size: Controls the overall arc system scale.
  • Arc Thickness: Adjusts the thickness of the electric arcs.
  • Material: Assigns the material used by the arcs.
  • Flicker Speed: Controls how fast arcs flicker and refresh.
  • Arc Speed Min: Sets the minimum arc movement speed.
  • Arc Speed Max: Sets the maximum arc movement speed.
  • Seed: Generates different random arc patterns.
