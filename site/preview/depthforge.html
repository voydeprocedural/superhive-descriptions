DepthForge
AI Image & Video to 3D

Transform images and videos into animated, fully editable 3D reliefs using locally powered AI and Blender Geometry Nodes.

DepthForge gives your images a new dimension. A photograph becomes a sculptural surface, and a video becomes moving geometry.

It combines AI depth estimation with procedural Geometry Nodes, unlocking creative 2.5D workflows without complicated setup or cloud processing. Everything runs locally on your computer, with no API keys and no cloud.

01 — IMAGE TO 3D
One image, real geometry

Import a photo, illustration or artwork. DepthForge analyzes its visual structure and creates a depth-driven 3D relief that you can shape directly inside Blender. Three depth modes are available.

AI Quality
Detailed depth estimation using Depth Anything V2 Small.
AI Fast
A lightweight model for faster processing.
Luminance
Converts brightness into height without AI inference.
02 — VIDEO TO ANIMATED 3D
Your footage, in motion, in 3D

Why stop at a single frame? DepthForge transforms video footage into animated depth geometry, so your relief evolves with the original motion.

Pro workflow: process a video once, then adjust its animated relief with live Geometry Nodes controls.

◆Video Depth Anything
AI video depth with temporal modeling.
◆Per-frame mode
Alternative per-frame AI processing with temporal smoothing.
◆Asynchronous processing
Runs with a progress display and can be cancelled.
◆16-bit depth sequences
Animated 16-bit depth image sequences.
◆Video on the relief
The original video is mapped onto the resulting geometry.
◆Clip control
Custom clip ranges and optional scene FPS matching.
03 — NON-DESTRUCTIVE WORKFLOW
Every detail under your control

DepthForge isn't a one-click conversion with a fixed result. Every relief has a procedural Geometry Nodes modifier, giving you control over its shape and appearance.

Modifier controls
Height, Size, Resolution, Black / White Point, Gamma, Smooth, Invert, Cutoff, Stretch Cut and Thickness.
Creative presets
Photo 2.5D, Bas-Relief, Cutout and Terrain.

07 — GETTING STARTED
Install, import, create
1Download the package matching your OS and Blender version.
2In Blender, go to Preferences > Get Extensions > Install from Disk.
3Open the 3D Viewport sidebar (N) and the DepthForge tab.
4Select an image or video and generate your relief.
5Customize the result with live Geometry Nodes controls.
08 — DETAILS
Compatibility & requirements
Requirements
◆Blender 4.2 and newer, installed with Install from Disk
◆Windows x64, macOS (Apple Silicon or Intel) and Linux x64
◆CPU inference with a local ONNX Runtime
◆Select the package for your operating system and Blender version
◆About 4-5 GB of free memory for high-resolution video depth estimation
◆macOS 14+ for direct video decoding on Apple Silicon; image sequences work on older versions
Good to know
◆AI depth estimation produces relative depth, not metric-accurate geometry.
◆Results vary with image content, motion, lighting and depth ambiguity.
◆The video model prioritizes temporal consistency but can't guarantee flicker-free output for every scene.
◆Processing speed depends on your CPU, available memory, input resolution and clip length.
