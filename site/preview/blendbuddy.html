BlendBuddy
Your Pixel Companion for Blender

A tiny pixel pet that lives in your viewport, helps you with Blender and tells you when your render is done, on your desktop and on your phone.

Blender sessions can be long and lonely. BlendBuddy gives you a little friend who walks and hops all over your viewport, plays with its buddies, reacts when you pet it, fixes common scene problems in one click and pops out on your desktop the moment a long render finishes.

New in 1.7: turn yourself, a friend or your pet into a buddy with Photo Pet.

01 — CHARACTERS
30 hand-made pixel characters

Fox, Dragon, Panda, Frog, Ember, Robot, Bunny, Moon, Pumpkin and many more. Every character has front, side and back views, so it walks, runs, turns around and naps.

The Halloween Edition adds 10 spooky extras, for 40 characters in total.

02 — PHOTO PET · NEW IN 1.7
Turn yourself or your pet into a buddy

Copy the prompt from the Photo Pet panel and paste it into ChatGPT, or any image AI that works from a photo, together with a photo. Pick the picture it draws, and BlendBuddy turns it into a real pixel character with front, side and back views, just like the built-in ones.

Your buddy walks, does tricks, changes color, wears accessories and can be a friend too. Keep as many as you like under My Characters.

BlendBuddy never sends your photo anywhere. You give it to the AI yourself, and only the finished pixel character is kept.

04 — PET IT
A different reaction every time

Click your pet (or the heart button) and it does a random trick: hop, roll, backflip, wiggle, spin, dance, zoomies, squish or a shower of hearts. It never does the same one twice in a row.

05 — MOVEMENT
Drag it anywhere

Your pet walks and hops all over the viewport. Hold and drag it anywhere: its legs run while you carry it, and it leaves a sparkle or wind trail in its own color.

Prefer a calm viewport? Keep it on the bottom edge, or choose Stay Put and it stays where you drop it.

06 — FRIENDS
Bring friends

Add up to 3 extra buddies, picked by you or at random. They say hi, play tag, do tricks together, play follow-the-leader, leapfrog over each other, throw little dance parties and nap together when you take a break. Click any of them to pet it, or drag it around.

07 — SOUNDS
Sounds and music

Every trick, jump, landing, pick-up and drop has its own little sound. Turn on soft footsteps, or the looping 8-bit song from the video with its own volume.

Footsteps and music are off by default. All sounds can be turned down or off.

08 — SCENE DOCTOR
Click for help

Stuck? Click your pet. It checks your scene for common problems like these and fixes many of them with one click. Use Ask me… to search quick answers to everyday Blender questions.

◆No active camera◆Unapplied scale◆Missing (pink) textures◆No lights and a dark world (black render)
09 — RENDER ALERTS
Never miss a render
◆Desktop pet (Windows)
Send your pet out of Blender and onto your desktop. It stays wherever you drop it, and a double-click makes it fly up or fall down. If Blender is in the background when a render finishes, your pet pops up above the taskbar to tell you.
◆Desktop notification
A notification and a cute sound when a render or bake finishes.
◆Phone notifications
Through Discord, every finished render arrives on your phone with a picture, the file name and how long it took.
◆Only long renders
Get notified only for renders longer than a set time, and choose whether bakes count too.
10 — PHONE NOTIFICATIONS
Discord setup in about a minute

A step-by-step guide is also built into the add-on, under How do I get the link?

1In Discord, create a private server just for you (free).
2Open a channel's settings > Integrations > Webhooks > New Webhook > Copy Webhook URL.
3In Blender, go to N panel > BlendBuddy > Phone Notifications and press the paste button.
4Press Send Test Message. That's it!
11 — INSTALLATION
How to install
1Download blendbuddy-1.7.0.zip, or blendbuddy-1.7.0-halloween.zip for the Halloween Edition.
2In Blender, go to Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions, open the dropdown menu, choose Install from Disk… and pick the zip.
3Open the 3D Viewport sidebar (N) and the BlendBuddy tab. Say hi to your new buddy!
12 — DETAILS
Requirements
Requirements
◆Blender 4.2 or newer (installs as a Blender extension)
◆Desktop pet: Windows. Everything else works on Windows, macOS and Linux.
◆Phone notifications: a free Discord account, with Allow Online Access turned on in Preferences > System > Network
◆Photo Pet: an image AI that can draw from a photo, such as ChatGPT
Good to know
◆Your Photo Pet characters stay when you install an update over the old version. Removing BlendBuddy deletes them; the folder button in My Characters lets you back them up.
◆Your webhook link is saved only in Blender's preferences on your computer. It's never sent anywhere except your own Discord channel.
◆Phone notifications use Discord, a third-party service. If you're active in Discord on your PC, Discord may hold the phone alert until you're away. The built-in guide shows how to change that.
◆BlendBuddy never blocks Blender. If the internet or Discord is down, your render isn't affected, and the panel tells you what went wrong.
