Grounded
Smart Scatter for Blender

Dress any terrain with plants, rocks and litter in a few clicks, and keep your viewport fast.

Grounded scatters grass, flowers, bushes, rocks, leaves and props over any surface. Pick a ready-made biome, let Grounded sort your own assets into a layered ecosystem, or paint where things grow.

Simple rules decide where each layer appears, and the layers relate to each other. Flowers fill the gaps in the grass, ferns clump against fallen logs, leaves pile up at walls and pebbles settle in hollows.

Everything runs on Geometry Nodes inside your file, so scenes render without the add-on.

02 — KEY FEATURES
A whole environment from one panel
◆Biomes
Wildflower meadow, forest floor, rocky slope, desert bloom and autumn street, each built from linked layers you can change.
◆Auto Ecosystem
Select any assets and the surface; Grounded sorts them into trees, shrubs, ground cover, rocks and litter, sets density from their size and links the layers.
◆Rules
Slope, height, facing direction, natural patches, shared clearings, vertex group and image masks, material filter, keep away from objects, clear paths along curves.
◆Layers that relate
Grass keeps clear of trunks and rocks, and any layer can grow toward another or keep away from it.
◆Gathering
Leaves at the base of walls, pebbles in hollows and corners, litter under trees, weeds along path edges.
◆Natural variation
Weight each asset to make it common or rare. Dryness turns plants yellow in patches, and every plant comes out a little lighter or darker than its neighbour.
◆Paint brush
Paint where a layer grows, erase it anywhere, or place single instances under the cursor.
◆Viewport budget
One number caps what the viewport shows; renders always get the full density. Camera culling and thinning help on very large scenes.
◆Living wind
One wind for the whole scene. Gusts sweep across the ground in the wind direction, grass bends more than trees, leaves flutter. On by default in every biome.
◆Bake and share
Save your layers as your own biome, turn a layer into a real mesh, export every instance to CSV for game engines, copy settings between layers.
03 — BRUSH
Paint where it grows

Paint a layer onto the ground, erase it anywhere, or place single instances under the cursor. Strokes live in hidden helper objects, so your model never changes and any mesh resolution works.

04 — RULES
Every rule stays live

Drag a setting and the layer follows in the viewport. Slope, height, facing direction, patches, masks and clear paths stack up, and every rule has a soft edge.

05 — GATHERING
Litter goes where it would really end up

Real ground isn't random. Leaves blow into corners and against walls, gravel rolls into ditches, needles fall under trees. Grounded's gathering rules make a layer denser in those places, or let it appear only there.

In the Autumn Street biome maple and oak leaves pile up in the gutter and along the walls, litter collects at the curb and weeds grow only in the cracks. Pick your own walls with one click.

06 — LAYERS THAT RELATE
Ground that looks grown, not sprinkled

Plants come in groups. One layer can grow toward another or keep away from it, and Bare Ground opens clearings at the same spots on every layer, so grass, flowers and clover leave the same ground free.

Blend Ground turns the soil into damp humus under the leaves, with bare earth between the drifts.

07 — NATURAL VARIATION
Dry patches, not one flat green

Dryness turns plants yellow in patches and leaves flowers and bark alone. Every plant comes out a little lighter or darker than its neighbour, and the undersides of leaves come out paler.

08 — LIVING WIND
One wind for the whole scene

Gusts sweep across the ground in the wind direction, grass bends more than trees and leaves flutter. It is on by default in every biome and keeps working without the add-on.

12 — INSTALLATION
Up and running in a minute
1Download the zip from your Superhive library. Do not unzip it.
2In Blender open Edit, Preferences, Get Extensions, the menu at the top right, Install from Disk, and pick the zip.
3Open the sidebar in the 3D viewport with N and click the Grounded tab.
4Select your ground, pick a biome and click Add Biome. For your own assets, select them, then the ground last, and click Scatter Selected or Auto Ecosystem.
13 — FAQ
Questions
Do I need Grounded to render my scenes?
No. Every setting lives in a Geometry Nodes modifier saved in your file, so it renders the same on a render farm or another computer.
Will it slow down my viewport?
The viewport budget caps what the viewport shows (400,000 by default, shared between layers) while renders get everything. Camera culling, thinning and box or point display help on very large scenes.
Which Blender versions are supported?
Blender 5.0 and newer. It is tested on 5.0, 5.1 and 5.2.
Can I use my own assets?
Yes. Select any meshes or collection instances, then the surface, and use Scatter Selected or Auto Ecosystem. Each asset keeps its own rotation and scale.
Does it work with Cycles and EEVEE?
Yes, the result is regular instances that both engines render.
Can I use the included assets commercially?
Yes. The included models and textures are CC0 (Poly Haven, ambientCG) or made by Voyde Procedural, free to use in commercial work.
Can I make my own biomes?
Yes. Set up layers on a surface, click Save as Biome, and it appears in the biome picker in every file. Copy its two files to share it.
